## 1. Project Overview

Delivery cost optimization is an important challenge in modern logistics and transportation systems. Increasing fuel prices, vehicle maintenance expenses, labor costs, and delivery delays affect the overall operational expenses of delivery services.

This project presents a mathematical optimization approach using **one-dimensional maxima–minima techniques** to identify the minimum delivery cost under a selected operating condition.

The proposed approach represents total delivery expenses as a mathematical cost function involving fixed costs, variable transportation costs, and time-dependent penalties.

By applying differentiation and maxima–minima analysis, the project identifies critical points and determines the minimum-cost operating condition.

The approach provides a simple mathematical framework for improving delivery planning and reducing unnecessary expenses.

## 2. Problem Statement

Delivery operations frequently experience increased expenses due to inefficient route planning, excessive fuel consumption, improper scheduling, and poor utilization of transportation resources.

Traditional delivery planning methods may depend on manual decisions and experience, which do not always produce the minimum possible cost.

Therefore, a simple mathematical optimization technique is needed to analyze delivery expenses and identify the most economical operating condition.

This project addresses the problem by developing a one-dimensional cost function and applying first- and second-order derivative tests to determine the minimum delivery expense.

## 4. Proposed Methodology

The project follows a mathematical modeling and analytical optimization approach.

### Step 1: Identify the Decision Variable

Select one important variable that influences delivery expenses, such as:

- Delivery distance.
- Number of delivery trips.
- Vehicle speed.
- Shipment size.
- Delivery frequency.

### Step 2: Develop the Cost Function

Represent the total delivery expense using a continuous mathematical function.

The general form is:

\[
C(x)=\text{Total Delivery Cost}
\]

Where:
- \(C(x)\) represents the total delivery expense.
- \(x\) represents the selected decision variable.

### Step 3: Find Critical Points

Calculate the first derivative of the cost function:

\[
C'(x)=0
\]

The solutions represent possible critical points.

### Step 4: Apply the Second Derivative Test

Calculate the second derivative:

\[
C''(x)
\]

If:

\[
C''(x)>0
\]

the critical point represents a local minimum.

If:

\[
C''(x)<0
\]

the critical point represents a local maximum.

### Step 5: Analyze the Results

Evaluate the cost function at the identified minimum point and compare it with other operating conditions to understand the potential cost savings.

## 6. One-Dimensional Maxima–Minima Optimization

One-dimensional optimization focuses on a single decision variable to simplify the mathematical analysis of a complex problem.

The maxima–minima method uses derivatives to determine the points where a function reaches minimum or maximum values.

In this project, the technique is applied to delivery expenses to identify the operating condition that minimizes total cost.

The approach offers the following advantages:

- Simple mathematical formulation.
- Reduced computational complexity.
- Easy identification of critical points.
- Clear interpretation of optimization results.
- Practical application in small- and medium-scale delivery operations.
